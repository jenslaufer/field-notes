## Recurring themes

### 1. Distribution is the binding constraint, not building
- Building is cheap; the edge is the gotcha, the funnel and reach. FK/HC calculators, extensions and sites have earned ~0; the factories are several layers deep, the customer is missing ([08-12](2026-08-12-ai-agent-workforce-allie-k-miller.md), [07-29](2026-07-29-software-is-dead-unfair-advantage.md), [06-15](2026-06-15-making-money-with-ios-apps.md)).
- Steps 3–5 of any agent playbook (shadow, spec, build) are home turf; steps 1, 2, 6, 7 (niche, pilots, distribution) are the wall ([07-01](2026-07-01-ai-agents-are-the-new-saas.md), [07-27](2026-07-27-marketing-agents-too-good-cody-schneider.md)).
- Strangers named the constraint twice in one week: "build everything, sell nothing" ([08-25](2026-08-25-sip-live-support-eats-engineering-jonathan-courtney.md)); "messages sent is activity" fails here — ~500 users and 17 checkout sessions in five months, 1 click in 28 days on the landing domains ([08-31](2026-08-31-marketing-engineer-growth-repo-six-systems.md)).
- Do not build artefact N+1. "Week two: execute, no tinkering" and the 100-day lesson say the same ([08-21](2026-08-21-grok-bot-agent-team-newsletter-billy-howell.md), [08-03](2026-08-03-graph-engineering-clearly-explained.md)).
- Only routes that work without a network count: the product sends itself to the next person ([09-24](2026-09-24-muse-connectors-app-store-moment.md)), timeless long-form search on owned topics with a kill number ([09-21](2026-09-21-write-with-ai-commodity-personality-original-nicolas-cole.md)), paid search/ads ([07-01](2026-07-01-ai-agents-are-the-new-saas.md)).

### 2026-08-21 · [Grok Bot: How to Build a 1-Person $10M Company](2026-08-21-grok-bot-agent-team-newsletter-billy-howell.md)
- Do not buy Grok Bot. Its 200–300 USD/month stack runs here on the Max plan: assistant daemon = chief of staff, `state/routines.md` = routines, agent-tasks + Fabrik = specialists, Telegram briefing = five-line report. Its one edge: cloud VM vs the mini-PC single point of failure — addressed by `watchdog.sh` and `alert-email.sh`, not by a subscription.
- Four missing practices:
  1. Earn the agent: do the task once, then build. Most-used tools (`investments` 30, `telegram` 8, `buchhaltung` 7) grew from repeated manual work; the 133 never-called were built on suspicion.
  2. Week two: execute, no tinkering — same as the 100-day lesson.
