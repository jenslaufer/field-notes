### 2026-05-13 · [The Shape of the Company as the AI Moat](2026-05-13-shape-of-the-company-as-ai-moat.md)

- **Moat ≠ features** — same lesson as the iOS-apps and "9 opportunities" notes from another angle: when building is free, the durable edge is structural (distribution there, *organizational shape* here). For **Fabrik** (a sellable product) the defensible story is not "we ship code with agents" (copyable) but the **operating shape**: operator-reviewed autonomous engineering, made + operated in Germany. A *form* claim, not a feature claim — lean into it in positioning.
- **"Form must mirror story" applies to the 1-human-1-AI shape.** Jens's moat is no single calculator or site; it is the *closed-loop operating structure* (assistant + agent-tasks + fabrik) that lets one person run a portfolio. The honest marketing frame is structural, not a feature list.
- **"Chosen vs Seen"** for any future hire/collaborator: pay in tangible stakes, never in "belonging + Later". Also a copywriting lens: landing pages selling *being seen* (real outcomes, authority) convert ambitious buyers better than pages selling *being chosen* (vague community).
- **"What kind of person can only be themselves here?"** — a positioning question for solytics/FK/HC audiences: design the product so a specific user feels uniquely served, rather than chasing a broader, blander fit.
