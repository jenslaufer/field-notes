### 2026-03-18 · [Overcoming Negativity Bias Through Tiny Experiments](2026-03-18-anne-laure-lecunff-negativity-bias-tiny-experiments.md)

*For the concept-simulator / tiny-experiments microsite:*

- This *is* the source material behind `jenslaufer.com/tiny-experiments/`. The episode reframes the Taleb optionality argument in neuroscience terms: negativity bias is the *psychological* reason people under-expose themselves to convex bets. "Tiny experiments" = bounded downside, open upside = a **barbell of the self**.
- Hook for a future *Uncertainty* or *Self-Experiment* simulator: users run a PACT ("I will X for N trials"), the simulator draws noisy outcomes and *shows* that judging any single trial is the trap — only the distribution over trials carries signal.
