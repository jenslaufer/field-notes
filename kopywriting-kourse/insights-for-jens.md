## By episode

### 2026-06-25 · [Tim Soulo of Ahrefs — Massive shifts in SEO, AEO](2026-06-25-tim-soulo-ahrefs-seo-aeo-shifts.md)

1. **Brand before search.** The products have no brand → no branded search → they lean on generic SEO, exactly the channel that is dying (FK/HC = evergreen YMYL that Google now answers itself). This explains *why* FK/HC are traffic-dead beyond "young domain": they target amalgamatable evergreen terms with no brand pulling people.
2. **Free, instant, no-signup tools are a proven traction engine — and Jens already has the asset.** Ahrefs bootstrapped on a free instant report; their best affiliates were free tools. Direct endorsement of **embeddable mini-tools (channel #4)** and **fingrab**. The concept-simulators and calculators have the right shape; missing is the brand/notice layer that turns a user into a searcher.
3. **Serve the tiny branded searches.** fingrab has installs + 3 paying subs + a name people type. Serve the query "fingrab" perfectly (store listing, a "how to use fingrab" page); fulfilling it grows it via word of mouth. Same for "Jens Laufer".
4. **Get noticed by one real, slightly-crazy, documented thing** — not by posting nicely. Same conclusion as [`distribution-without-audience.md`](../distribution-without-audience.md) and the Greg-Isenberg "skill 2" thread, now confirmed by a $100M operator. Stunt → notice → branded search → SEO/AEO serves it.
5. **Build engines, not campaigns.** Cross-check on the **KI-Sprint paid-search** debate (waiting #75): a paid campaign is a burst with a hard ceiling; an engine (concept-sim family, a real blog, a free tool) compounds. Bias the runway toward engines.
6. **Optimize for being mentioned (AEO).** For solytics/e-invoice the future lever is brand/product mentions across the web, so the product surfaces in AI answers about e-Rechnung. Links alone won't carry it.
7. **Don't over-trust attribution.** Pick channels on fit + own strengths + feedback speed (the existing filter), not on a clean ROI number social media won't give anyway.
8. **Caution on the calculator thesis.** The calc bet holds only where the tool is *specific, embedded, and branded* — not "yet another generic Rechner" a user could ask ChatGPT for.
