## By episode

### 2026-09-06 · [Career Hound: a boring product that wins entirely on distribution](2026-09-06-career-hound-boring-product-wins-on-distribution.md)

1. **"Proven category, blame the distribution" is a cleaner version of a decision Jens already makes badly.** The repeated pattern: build artefact N+1, then discover nobody finds it. The filter inverts the diagnosis in advance: enter only where demand is proven, so silence can only mean the distribution is wrong.
2. **His "timeless format" and the measured store law are the same law.** Measured on PlaylistGrab (03.08.) and again on PDFuse (13.09.): Chrome Web Store rank follows the listing text, not popularity — PDFuse ranks on `splitting merging pdf` and nowhere on `split pdf`. Roman's version: find the words the audience already uses, then repeat them forever. The store listing is a timeless format, written once, earning for years.
3. **The $20-a-video creator channel is a distribution test never run, and the cheapest one in the note.** No audience, no list. Ten videos against a written format sheet cost roughly $200 and give a real read on whether anyone wants the thing — far below any ad budget.
4. **The format sheet, not the creator, is the asset.** If tested, build first the instruction sheet that leaves no room for confusion; outreach comes second.
5. **Counterweight — do not read $28K as $28K.** MRR $12K, churn 31 %, LTV $50. The business needs 11.7 million views per month to stand still. Not passive income. Against Jens's stated goal (cashflow, as passive as possible), this engine sits at the opposite end of the axis: high throughput, high maintenance, no compounding asset except the format sheet.
