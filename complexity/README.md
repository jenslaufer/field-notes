# Complexity Science

Notes on complexity science, complex systems, emergence and their foundations — the Santa Fe Institute canon (David Krakauer, Melanie Mitchell, John Krakauer), systems thinking, stochastic processes, and antifragility. Each note ends with a solo-builder read.

- [`complexity-science.md`](complexity-science.md) — David Krakauer (Santa Fe Institute): what complexity science is, the four pillars (entropy, evolution, dynamics, computation), why emergence makes every science beyond physics necessary
- [`landscape-of-21st-century-science.md`](landscape-of-21st-century-science.md) — David Krakauer, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E1 (2019): why grand unified theories failed, the 21st-century schism between complexity science (understanding) and machine learning (prediction), mountains/monasteries/metropolis, risk as a vector
- [`rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md`](rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md) — David Krakauer, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E26 (April 2020, Transmission Series Ep. 1): why alignment across scales is fragility, why bad data demands the simplest model, good scientists are comfortable with uncertainty, superlinear group scaling, incremental change is the only real change
- [`crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md`](crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md) — David Krakauer, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E29 (April 2020, Transmission Series Ep. 3), read through Taleb's antifragility: the virus as a quasi-species with its own error threshold, mass extinction as creative destruction, why your neighbor beats the newspaper, and Bill Miller on high beta vs. true convexity when buying at the point of maximum pessimism
- [`embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md`](embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md) — David Krakauer, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E31 (May 2020, Transmission Series Ep. 5), read through Taleb's antifragility: why averaging R-naught hides the tail, testing capacity as an instrumentation trap, conviction narratives as both antifragility's answer and its own failure mode, frailty as lost redundancy, and why Rome fell to two stacked shocks not one
- [`exponentials-economics-ecology.md`](exponentials-economics-ecology.md) — David Krakauer, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E32 (May 2020, Transmission Series Ep. 6), read through Taleb's antifragility: the uncertainty of ignorance vs. the uncertainty of the Butterfly Effect (the split antifragility.md leaves implicit), R-naught as an interest rate, superlinear city scaling, mechanism design as skin-in-the-game engineering, and the Galápagos tortoise that forgot how to be afraid
- [`cultural-evolution-songbirds.md`](cultural-evolution-songbirds.md) — Nicole Creanza, COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E22 (March 2020): partial connectivity beats full connectivity, why useful tools get lost in small populations, population size predicts cultural complexity only in food-producing societies, opportunity as the mother of invention, open- vs closed-ended song learning
- [`geoffrey-west-scaling-cities.md`](geoffrey-west-scaling-cities.md) — Geoffrey West (physicist, former SFI president, *Scale*), all four COMPLEXITY appearances summarized individually — S1:E35+E36 (2020), S1:E74 with David Krakauer (2021), S2:E1 Physics of Life (2024): sublinear infrastructure vs superlinear social output, why there is no Godzilla and cities break up instead, the finite-time singularity and the accelerating innovation treadmill, innovation ≠ technology, report deciling not doubling — and West's own correction that the urban dividend only reaches the top decile while the bottom scales linearly
- [`sabine-hauert-swarming-across-scales.md`](sabine-hauert-swarming-across-scales.md) — Sabine Hauert (swarm engineering, University of Bristol), COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E3 (October 2019): cheap agents are necessarily noisy and dumb, no agent can see the system-level goal it is scored on, the field's own retraction that most swarm algorithms break at the numbers they are advertised for, the two opposite routes to robustness (dumb agents or an artificial immune system), steering a swarm you did not design, and the reversal at the end — ten years of explaining replaced by use case studies
- [`melanie-moses-scaling-decentralization.md`](melanie-moses-scaling-decentralization.md) — Melanie Moses (computer science, UNM; SFI external faculty), Scaling Theory #21 (July 2025): decentralization changes the scaling law but its infrastructure is never free (the swarm-robot roadway), information scales differently from energy so filters are load-bearing, the ants that ignore the pheromone trail are the adaptive ones, no universal scaling law but keep looking, and the AI leverage sits in the protocol that connects flawed agents — not in the agent
- [`nature-of-intelligence.md`](nature-of-intelligence.md) — Melanie Mitchell's 6-part COMPLEXITY season (Santa Fe Institute): each episode summarized individually — intelligence as a suitcase word, language vs thought, LLMs as "exotic mind-like entities" that role-play, babies vs machines, construct validity in testing, Mitchell's own AI-seasons worldview
- [`john-krakauer-brain-mind.md`](john-krakauer-brain-mind.md) — John Krakauer (neurologist, Johns Hopkins; brother of David), COMPLEXITY podcast S1:E95+E96 (2022): start neuroscience from behavior not neurons, no privileged level of explanation, the Learning Salon, human meaning-making as a discontinuity of kind not degree
- [`thinking-in-systems.md`](thinking-in-systems.md) — Donella Meadows: stocks & flows, feedback loops, systems traps & the 12 leverage points
- [`stochastic-processes.md`](stochastic-processes.md) — stochastic processes outside physics: the eight-process zoo (random walk, Markov, Poisson, branching, martingales, multiplicative growth, mean reversion, Pólya urn), the five transferable concepts (ergodicity, stationarity, Markov property, absorbing states, tails)
- [`antifragility.md`](antifragility.md) — Taleb's antifragile triad, convexity, barbell & via negativa, with a solo-builder playbook
