# Complexity Science — insights for Jens

This file collects the personal takeaways that were previously inside the individual notes.

## Recurring themes

**1. Thin data licenses only the crudest model.**
- Sparse data (2 clicks in 28 days, four leads, one paid channel without a verdict) calls for the crudest estimator. The FK/HC shutdown on three number pairs was the right size of model. Do not upgrade it. ([Rigorous uncertainty](2020-04-06-rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md), [Cultural evolution](2020-03-12-cultural-evolution-songbirds.md))
- Ask for the distribution, not the mean, before calling a channel dead or alive (`learning_cold_start_number_needs_its_distribution.md`). Ask what the null model would show before trusting a zero. ([Embracing complexity](2020-05-04-embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md))
- Sort each call: ignorance-shaped uncertainty shrinks with data; amplification-shaped does not — cap the downside instead. ([Two kinds of uncertainty](2020-05-11-exponentials-economics-ecology.md))
- The fingrab verdict rule is Poisson thinking: set the kill threshold in expected conversions, not gut-feel days. ([Stochastic processes](stochastic-processes.md))

**2. The stack is aligned — one shock takes everything.**
- One mini-PC (assistant, agent runners, Fabrik), one Anthropic subscription, one distribution hypothesis (organic search). Same mechanism at every scale. ([Rigorous uncertainty](2020-04-06-rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md), [Two kinds of uncertainty](2020-05-11-exponentials-economics-ecology.md))
- The LiteLLM proxy is the engineered misalignment. It is "not yet wired into the agents" — finish the wiring. ([Embracing complexity](2020-05-04-embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md), [Crisis and creative opportunity](2020-04-20-crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md))
- Add the combination question: which two single risks, landing in the same week, would sink the operation? ([Embracing complexity](2020-05-04-embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md), [Antifragility](antifragility.md))
- A long clean run is a question, not an answer: silently expiring creds, the SSH lockout, the dead calendar sync. ([Two kinds of uncertainty](2020-05-11-exponentials-economics-ecology.md))

**3. Agent setup: small-number rules, an immune system, a separate judge.**
- Agent-task and Fabrik rules (max 3 open PRs per repo, one new PR per repo per night) are right — and do not scale. Name what was measured at the new number before running more agents. ([Hauert](2019-10-16-sabine-hauert-swarming-across-scales.md))
- The watchdog stack is the artificial immune system, not overhead. Actor/critic and quality-gate keep decider and judge apart. The protocol between agents beats a better single model. ([Hauert](2019-10-16-sabine-hauert-swarming-across-scales.md), [Two kinds of uncertainty](2020-05-11-exponentials-economics-ecology.md), [Moses](2025-07-29-melanie-moses-scaling-decentralization.md))
- Fluency is not understanding. A correct diff is a correlate, not comprehension. The QG gates are applied construct validity. ([Nature of Intelligence](2024-09-25-nature-of-intelligence.md), [John Krakauer](2022-11-11-john-krakauer-brain-mind.md))
- Confident reports — the assistant's, an agent's, mine — supply certainty without reducing uncertainty. ([Rigorous uncertainty](2020-04-06-rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md), [Embracing complexity](2020-05-04-embracing-complexity-systemic-interventions.md))

**4. The bottleneck is distribution, not artefact N+1.**
- Isolation produces unique solutions; the payoff comes at contact. The missing middle is the monastery: owned list (#60), LinkedIn shots (#67). ([Landscape](2019-10-09-landscape-of-21st-century-science.md), [Cultural evolution](2020-03-12-cultural-evolution-songbirds.md))
- Four months of calculators at four clicks a month: being on the platform is the linear decile. ([Geoffrey West](2020-06-17-geoffrey-west-scaling-cities.md))
- Hauert stopped explaining and started asking. 100 days of building, ~0 new cashflow — and no use case studies run. ([Hauert](2019-10-16-sabine-hauert-swarming-across-scales.md))
- The relaunch reflex is the treadmill. One lever per week beats a redesign. ([Rigorous uncertainty](2020-04-06-rigorous-uncertainty-covid.md), [Crisis and creative opportunity](2020-04-20-crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md))

**5. Portfolio shape: barbell, variant cloud, a trail to exploit.**
- Two tracks (cashflow vs. explorer) are both legitimate; neither pays in the other's currency. ([Landscape](2019-10-09-landscape-of-21st-century-science.md))
- The product set is a quasi-species. Keep variants small; one bet past its error threshold vaporizes. ([Crisis and creative opportunity](2020-04-20-crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md), [Antifragility](antifragility.md))
- Gathering vs. constructed niche: only Launch Kit and the agent infrastructure compound; the extension portfolio is gathering. ([Cultural evolution](2020-03-12-cultural-evolution-songbirds.md))
- Too many wandering ants, no reinforced trail. Exploration is defensible only once something is exploited. ([Moses](2025-07-29-melanie-moses-scaling-decentralization.md))

**6. The concept sims are complementary cognitive artifacts.**
- Sims are the diffusion layer for complexity insights; "models over theories" is their pitch. ([Landscape](2019-10-09-landscape-of-21st-century-science.md), [Complexity science](complexity-science.md))
- Gaps worth building: emergence, gambler's ruin, Pólya urn, Poisson clumping. ([Complexity science](complexity-science.md), [Stochastic processes](stochastic-processes.md))
- Slop makes the participation-first sims a moat. ([Nature of Intelligence](2024-09-25-nature-of-intelligence.md))

**7. Slack and runway are inputs.**
- ~18 sessions a day suppresses invention causally. ([Cultural evolution](2020-03-12-cultural-evolution-songbirds.md))
- Keep the safe leg boring and overfunded — it buys the option to move. ([Crisis and creative opportunity](2020-04-20-crisis-and-creative-opportunity.md))
- The runway is a first-passage problem: which paths touch zero, and when. ([Stochastic processes](stochastic-processes.md), [Landscape](2019-10-09-landscape-of-21st-century-science.md))

### [Stochastic processes outside physics](stochastic-processes.md)
- The `/ergodicity/` simulator sits in the multiplicative-growth slot.
- Concepts family already covers half the zoo (/ergodicity/, /sequence/, /compounding/, feedback-loop sims, /uncertainty/). Gaps, each a one-evening build on the existing scaffold: gambler's ruin ("fair game, certain ruin"), Pólya urn ("two identical products, one ends with 80% share"), Poisson clumping ("which strip is random, which human-faked?").
- Fingrab verdict rule = Poisson thinking. ~30 clicks at small λ: weeks of zeros are the expected path. Fix a clean window, judge on one number; set the kill threshold in expected conversions (spend × plausible rate).
- Attention is a Pólya urn: cold start is structural. Two answers — buy first attention (paid search) or many cheap draws (serendipity lane, measured at the input). Both lanes of the Tuesday routine.
- Non-stationarity: "my last three applications all converted to permanent-position offers" — the freelance market's process changed; 2021 base rates are from a different process.
- Absorbing states frame the runway: the net-withdrawal Monte Carlo is first-passage. Spending flexibility moves the barrier — same expected spend, fewer absorbed paths.
